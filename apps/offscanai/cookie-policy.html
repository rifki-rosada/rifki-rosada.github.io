Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how OffScan AI (“we”, “our”, “the App”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at https://rifkirosada.com/apps/offscanai. OffScan AI is an OCR and AI-powered mobile application that extracts text from images securely and efficiently.

1. Do We Use Cookies?

The OffScan AI mobile app does not use cookies. All processing is executed locally on your device (OCR, AI extraction, PDF export, text formatting).

Cookies may be used only when you access our website or external support pages linked from the app.

2. What Types of Cookies May Be Used?

Essential Cookies

These cookies ensure the website functions correctly, including navigation, security, and loading speed.

Analytics Cookies

Anonymous analytics may be used to help us understand website performance, such as:

  • Page views
  • Device type and browser
  • Anonymous visitor counts
  • Performance metrics

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services may place cookies, including:

  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Google Fonts
  • Embedded documentation or external pages

3. Managing Cookies

You may block or delete cookies directly from your browser settings. Each browser provides cookie management tools including:

  • Clear cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Site-specific cookie permissions

4. Mobile App Tracking

OffScan AI does not use tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or any behavioral analytics. Your images and extracted text remain fully private and never leave your device unless you explicitly share them.
