Privacy Policy for OffScan AI

Last updated: November 26, 2025

OffScan AI (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides an AI-powered document scanning and OCR application designed to convert images into text using processing that happens directly on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

OffScan AI does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • We do not save your scanned images.
  • We do not save or upload OCR text.
  • We do not track personal identifiers or device data.
  • No analytics tools or third-party tracking SDKs are used.

2. How Your Data Is Used

All processing takes place locally on your device. Your images and OCR results remain fully under your control, and you decide when to save, copy, or delete them.

3. Network Usage

OffScan AI only uses internet connectivity for:

  • Downloading additional OCR language packs (optional).
  • Receiving app updates through Google Play.

No images or extracted text are ever uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers.

4. Third-Party Services

OffScan AI does not use external analytics, advertising networks, or cloud-based OCR services. Only Google Play services may operate as part of the standard Android and Google Play ecosystem.

5. Children’s Privacy

OffScan AI is intended for users aged 13 and above. Because we do not collect or store personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
