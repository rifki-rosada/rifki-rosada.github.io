Information to include

To help us understand and fix your issue as quickly as possible, please include the following details in your message:

  • Your device brand and model (for example: Pixel 7, Samsung A54).
  • Your Android version.
  • A short description of what you were doing when the issue occurred.
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording of the problem (if possible).
  • Whether the problem happens every time or only in specific cases.

We typically respond within 24–48 hours on business days.
